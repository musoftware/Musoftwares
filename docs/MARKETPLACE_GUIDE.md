# Service Marketplace Guide

## 1. Purchasing Productized Services

The Service Marketplace offers fixed-scope, productized service offerings categorized by industry (Web Development, UI/UX Design, DevOps Architecture).

```text
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Select Package Tier                                    │

│ ⚪ [Standard: Multi-Tenant App]         $1,499 | 14 Days│
│ ⚪ [Premium: Enterprise Monolith]       $2,499 | 21 Days│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [Secure Order via Escrow]   [Message Seller]           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Steps to Purchase a Service Package
1. Browse or search the marketplace catalog using keyword tags and category filters.
2. Select an offering to inspect package tiers (`Basic`, `Standard`, `Premium`). Compare delivery timeframes, revision allowances, and included feature checkboxes.
3. Review the seller's verified portfolio gallery and read client feedback ratings.
4. Select your desired package tier and click **[Secure Order via Escrow]**.
5. **Escrow Checkout:** Your wallet balance is verified and debited. Funds are safely locked in system escrow until you verify successful delivery. An isolated real-time order chat channel is created immediately for project collaboration.

## 2. Selling Productized Services

If you are an expert developer or designer, you can package your skills into fixed-price service offerings to generate recurring freelance income.

### Creating a Service Catalog Listing
1. Navigate to **Service Marketplace** > **My Services** and click **[+ Create Service]**.
2. Upload a professional cover image and gallery screenshots illustrating your past work.
3. Define your package tiers:
   - **Basic Tier:** Entry-level deliverables for clients with limited budgets.
   - **Standard Tier:** Your most popular, comprehensive service package.
   - **Premium Tier:** High-end, VIP enterprise delivery with expedited timelines and extended support.
4. Submit your service for moderation. Once reviewed and approved by platform administrators, your offering becomes live and discoverable in the public catalog.

## 3. Order Management & Dispute Resolution

Once an order is placed, both buyer and seller manage the project lifecycle from a dedicated order dashboard.

### Milestone Tracking & Deliveries
- **Seller Responsibility:** Deliver all promised features within the agreed timeframe. When ready, click **[Deliver Order]** and attach project archives or access credentials.
- **Buyer Verification:** Review the delivered work. If revisions are required within your package allowance, click **[Request Revision]**. When completely satisfied, click **[Sign Off & Complete Order]** to release escrow funds to the seller.

### How Our Escrow Protection & Dispute System Works
If communication breaks down or agreed deliverables are not met:
1. Either party can click **[Open Dispute]** from the order dashboard.
2. Order funds remain frozen in escrow vault holding while platform arbitration staff review chat transcripts, submitted project files, and package specifications.
3. Once reviewed, administrators will issue an equitable ruling, either refunding escrow funds to the buyer's wallet or releasing earned compensation to the seller.
