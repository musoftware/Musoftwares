# Platform Master User Guide

Welcome to the **MuSoftware ERP Platform**. This user manual provides step-by-step instructions for navigating your dashboard, managing client accounting, posting freelance jobs, and purchasing marketplace services.

## Platform Navigation Overview

When you log into your account, your top navigation bar provides instant access to the four interconnected modules of the platform:

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ MuSoftware SaaS    [ERP]  [Freelance]  [Marketplace]  [Chat (3)]  [User] │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **ERP Dashboard:** Manage your multi-tenant accounting, client invoices, timer items, and wallet ledgers.
- **Freelance Marketplace:** Post job requirements, match with verified skilled freelancers, evaluate bids, and manage smart contracts.
- **Service Marketplace:** Browse productized service tiers (Basic, Standard, Premium), inspect seller portfolios, and place escrow-backed orders.
- **Real-Time Chat:** A unified communication center displaying active chats across your contracts, orders, and support tickets.

## User Account Roles & Capabilities

Your interface options adjust automatically based on your assigned platform role:
1. **Client Role:** Can subscribe to ERP accounting plans, issue invoices, maintain client wallets, post freelance jobs, and purchase marketplace services.
2. **Freelancer Role:** Can showcase verified skills, maintain public portfolios, bid on active job postings using marketplace points, and deliver service orders.
3. **Super-Admin Role:** Oversees entire platform moderation, approves withdrawal requests, audits tenant ledgers, and can impersonate any user account for direct support.

## Security & Session Management

To ensure your financial records remain completely protected:
- Your session automatically locks after 120 minutes of inactivity.
- All password resets and sensitive account changes require email verification.
- When contacting support, our customer success team may utilize our secure **Impersonation System** to view your dashboard exactly as you experience it to help resolve issues quickly.
