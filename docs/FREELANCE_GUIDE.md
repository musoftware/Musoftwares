# Freelance Hiring & Bidding Guide

## 1. Posting Job Requirements (For Clients)

The Freelance module connects businesses with vetted expert professionals using automated skill matching and escrow-secured contracts.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Post Freelance Job        Points Cost to Publish: [5]  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Title: [Senior React UI Architect Needed             ] │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Budget: [$1,500 - $3,000 USD]   Type: [Fixed Price ▾]  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Required Skills: [React ✕] [Tailwind ✕] [TypeScript ✕] │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Steps to Publish a Job Posting
1. Navigate to **Freelance Marketplace** > **My Jobs** and click **[+ Post a Job]**.
2. Enter a descriptive title and detailed project specification.
3. Select required skills from our categorized taxonomy. The platform will automatically notify all registered freelancers who possess matching skill tags.
4. Set your budget range and project type (`Fixed Price` or `Hourly Rate`).
5. **Publishing Fee:** Publishing a job posting requires a nominal point deduction (e.g., 5 points) from your account balance to ensure high-quality marketplace listings.

---

## 2. Bidding on Active Jobs (For Freelancers)

To maintain an unspammed, professional bidding environment, submitting proposals requires marketplace bidding points.

### Submitting a Winning Proposal
1. Browse active job listings filtered by your verified skills.
2. Open a job specification and evaluate the client's budget and requirements.
3. Click **[Submit Proposal]**.
4. Enter your proposed price, estimated delivery timeframe (in days), and a customized cover letter detailing how you will solve the client's problem.
5. Review the required point cost to bid (e.g., 2 to 4 points) and click **[Confirm Bid]**.

### Managing Bidding Points
- All newly verified freelancer accounts receive a complimentary starting point balance.
- If your points balance runs low, you can instantly purchase point packages (e.g., 50 points for $10 USD) using funds stored in your platform wallet.

## 3. Proposal Evaluations & Contract Activation

When proposals are submitted on your job posting, you can evaluate candidate profiles, read reviews from past clients, and inspect portfolio showcases.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Evaluating Proposals: React UI Architect               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Candidate: Sarah Connor (★ 4.95 - 32 Jobs Completed)   │
│ Bid Amount: $2,400 USD | Delivery: 14 Days             │
│ [Accept & Start Escrow]   [Open Chat]   [Reject]       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Activating a Contract
1. Click **[Open Chat]** to clarify technical details with the freelancer in real time before making a final hiring decision.
2. Once you reach an agreement, click **[Accept & Start Escrow]**.
3. **Escrow Hold:** The platform automatically verifies your wallet balance and locks the agreed contract funds in an escrow vault. The freelancer is alerted that funds are safely secured and work can begin.

---

## 4. Work Delivery & Payment Sign-Off

Once the project milestones are achieved, the freelancer submits the work for client evaluation.

### Freelancer Delivery Steps
1. Open the active contract dashboard and click **[Submit Delivery]**.
2. Upload compressed project files, code archives, or deployment links along with a delivery summary note.

### Client Verification & Payment Release
1. Inspect the delivered materials. If revisions are required, discuss them directly inside the contract chat channel.
2. When fully satisfied, click **[Approve & Complete Contract]**.
3. Escrow funds are immediately released from vault holding and credited directly to the freelancer's wallet balance after deducting platform service fees.
