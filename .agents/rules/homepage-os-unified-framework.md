# Rule: Homepage OS (Universal UI Design Framework)

Use this rule as an authoritative, permanent reference when designing, auditing, or refactoring any homepage. The role is strictly a **filter and diagnostic strategist**, not a passive copywriter. The core objective is eliminating "Keyword Dumping" (putting technical features or raw unstructured information randomly on the page). Every design decision (layout, palette, section sequence, form friction) must derive from rigorous diagnosis, not default assumptions.

---

## 1. Phase 1: Diagnosis (Ask and Filter Before Designing)

### A. The Said vs. Meant Filter (Fixing Feature Dumping)
1. **What are all the technical features/buzzwords stated by the owner?**
   - **Decision:** These are strictly banned from the hero section. Move them to `/how-it-works` or `/architecture` for technical evaluators.
2. **What does the customer actually MEAN by requesting that feature?** Translate each technical term into real business value (e.g., "Git handover" = "I own my digital asset forever without ransom").
   - **Decision:** This translated business language is the ONLY vocabulary permitted in the hero and first two sections.

### B. Business Model & Audience
1. **Purchase Decision Velocity:** Fast impulse vs. deliberate consultative decision.
2. **Product Value:** Low/Medium ticket vs. High-Ticket requiring negotiation and deep trust.
3. **Closing Channel:** Direct automated checkout vs. human consultation (Call/WhatsApp).
4. **Frequency:** One-time purchase vs. recurring subscription.
5. **Customization Level:** Off-the-shelf vs. bespoke. Bespoke systems require an upfront scope discovery flow, not fixed price tags.
6. **Audience (B2B vs. B2C):** B2B requires higher data density, case studies, and ROI proof with sharp geometry (Radius 2-8px). B2C leans on emotional status with soft geometry (Radius 16-28px).
7. **Individual vs. Group Decision:** If a committee or partner is involved, provide a shareable summary or executive brief.
8. **Trust Sensitivity:** High risk (health, financial, legal, enterprise operations) demands upfront trust weapons placed immediately near the hero.
9. **Sales Cycle:** Minutes vs. weeks. Longer cycles require resume/save mechanisms.
10. **Market Differentiation:** Saturated commodity requiring price contrast vs. unique proprietary studio requiring authority focus.
11. **Traffic Source:** Paid ad search intent (needs immediate value punchline) vs. referral/organic (accepts deeper narrative context).

### C. Psychology & Fear
1. **First 5 Seconds Emotions:** Determines surface color palette, photography style, and tone of voice.
2. **The #1 Decision Blocker (Fear):** Must be dismantled directly in Section 2 immediately following the hero.
3. **The Unspoken Emotional Objection:** Determines the framing and authenticity of social proof.

### D. Behavioral Evidence
1. **Raw Behavioral Proof:** WhatsApp chats, real customer quotes, unedited testimonials.
2. **Reasons Behind Last 3 Lost Deals:** Determines elements that must be permanently deleted from the page.
3. **Most Repeated Pre-Payment Question:** Dictates the micro-copy placed beside the primary CTA.
4. **Recurring Praise Term:** Anchor keyword reiterated across section headers.

### E. Differentiation
1. **Tired Competitor Cliché:** The pattern intentionally broken to stand out.
2. **Market Leader vs. Challenger:** Dictates grid rigidity and visual boldness.
3. **The One Signature Interaction (Wow Moment):** Dedicated interactive tool (Estimator, Calculator, Architecture Matcher).
4. **Proprietary Moat:** The core capability competitors cannot match, featured as hero proof.

### F. Constraints & System Logic
1. **Browsing Profile:** Mobile bandwidth, viewport budget (360px minimum), and WCAG contrast (4.5:1 min, 7:1 for low vision).
2. **Visual Asset Availability:** Real production screenshots vs. clean icon systems vs. data cards.
3. **Language & Brand Boundaries:** RTL / LTR pathing, font hierarchy (e.g., Cairo/Inter), and chromatic guardrails.
4. **Bounce Countdown:** Hero height (60-75vh) and headline length limit (under 10 words).
5. **The Single Success Action:** Primary CTA geometry, position, and prominence.
6. **Next 10 Seconds Reality:** Button text accurately detailing the next step.
7. **Empty State Handling:** Never show empty blanks; render a compensatory registration CTA.
8. **Trust Weapons & Icebreaker:** Proof counters, live links, and interactive lead magnets.

---

## 2. Phase 2: Track Selection

### Track A: High-Ticket / Consultative (Deliberate Decision, Human Trust)
- Strictly forbid instant checkout or "Buy Now" carts in primary funnels.
- Every conversion path directs to human consultation (WhatsApp / Discovery Call / Scope Estimator).
- Form friction cap: Maximum 2-3 fields with explicit privacy assurances.

### Track B: Rapid Decision (Direct Transaction or Self-Serve Subscription)
- Clear direct checkout flow with secondary newsletter/resource capture.
- Primary CTA drives straight to checkout/onboarding without forced human scheduling.
- Trust built through ratings, money-back guarantees, and transparent pricing.

---

## 3. Phase 3: Inference Rules

| Condition | Architectural Decision |
|---|---|
| Serious / Enterprise Investment | Muted low-saturation palette + neutral deep backgrounds |
| Daily / Lifestyle Product | Saturated accents + natural daylight visuals |
| B2B / Operational Accuracy | Radius 2-8px + strict column grid + high data density |
| B2C / Emotional Experience | Radius 16-28px + soft ambient shadows |
| Cultural / Institutional Identity | Subtle micro-patterns at 3-7% opacity only |
| Tight Time / Executive Audience | High readability typography + line height 1.6-1.8 + scannable blocks |
| No Professional Photography | Precision vector icon system and product data cards |
| Authentic Production Photography | Real human captures with direct eye contact or real UI interfaces |
| Mobile Bandwidth Constraints | No background video autoplay + WebP formats + strict budget |
| Elderly / Low Vision Target | Base font 18px+ + contrast ratio 7:1+ |
| High-Ticket System | Micro-forms (max 2 fields) + clear privacy reassurance |
| Dominant Fear: Trust / Scam | Proof weapons positioned directly beneath the hero |
| Dominant Fear: Price / Commitment | Guarantees and transparent calculator positioned before pricing table |
| Technical Jargon in Hero | Automatically delete and relegate to secondary pages |
| Missing Category Data | Render proactive compensatory registration CTA, never blank blocks |

---

## 4. Phase 4: Scroll Sequence

1. **Hero:** Translated promise (MEANT, never Said) + single primary action + zero raw jargon.
2. **Destroy Biggest Fear:** Direct side-by-side or value contrast breaking the #1 hesitation.
3. **Bento / Pillars Grid:** 2 to 5 core capability pillars max with operational metrics.
4. **Trust Weapons:** Production proof gallery, case studies, live links, and verified metrics.
5. **Interactive Free Value Tool:** Scope estimator, price calculator, or diagnostic tool with baked-in CTA.
6. **Architect Authority & Final Close:** Personal founder/architect guarantee, SLA commitment, and rapid contact links.

---

## 5. Phase 5: Cognitive Load Minimization
- Every product or tier card must present essential specs and commercial reality in a single view.
- Sticky navigation tabs for instant multi-category jumping.
- Zero interfaces requiring more than two visual passes to understand available options.

---

## 6. Phase 6: Unified Action Protocol
- Single unified accent color reserved for primary conversion actions.
- Action button copy states the exact subsequent reality (e.g., "Calculate System Scope" or "Direct WhatsApp Consultation").
- Actions trigger lightweight modal dialogs or direct messaging, preventing full-page reload drop-offs.

---

## 7. Phase 7: Pitfall Checklist
- No empty states showing blank voids.
- No long form inquisitions (strictly 2-3 fields).
- No low-contrast muted gray or faint yellow copy.
- No raw technical jargon leaking into the hero.

---

## 8. Phase 8: Output Schema
Every proposed section must be documented using this standard schema:

**[Order]** + **[Design Decision]** + **[Logical Trace: Because Answer #X = Y, therefore Decision = Z]** + **[AIDA Stage: Attention / Interest / Desire / Action]**

---

## 9. Phase 9: Precise Visual Engineering
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Strict 8pt grid progression (8px, 16px, 24px, 48px, 96px).
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Headline promise -> contrast proof -> capability bento -> verified evidence -> conversion close.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Minimum 44px touch targets on mobile with sticky bottom bar when appropriate.
- **Contrast Integrity:** WCAG AA/AAA compliant (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy, 3:1 for large display).

---

## 10. Phase 10: Anti-AI Footprint Rules
- Strictly forbid random purple/pink mesh gradients or ungrounded glassmorphism.
- Strictly forbid vague marketing fluff ("transforming futures", "unmatched quality"). Use verifiable metrics and exact timelines.
- Strictly forbid floating elements unanchored to the 12-column grid.
- Strictly forbid identical repeating cards. Always visually emphasize the featured primary option.
- Strictly enforce production-grade edge states (empty states, validation errors, loading skeletons).
